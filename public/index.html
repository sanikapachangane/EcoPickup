The right stream matters

Waste categories

Choose the right collection stream for a safer and more efficient pickup.

Dry Waste

Paper, cardboard, packaging, and other recyclable dry materials.

  • Newspapers
  • Boxes
  • Packaging sheets

Wet Waste

Food scraps and organic waste that requires timely disposal.

  • Fruit peels
  • Cooked leftovers
  • Garden clippings

Plastic Waste

Plastic containers, wrappers, and recyclable material suitable for sorting.

  • Plastic bottles
  • Packaging films
  • Containers

E-Waste

Electrical items that should be processed with care and compliance.

  • Laptops
  • Chargers
  • Keyboards

Hazardous Waste

Potentially risky material that requires specialized handling.

  • Batteries
  • Paint cans
  • Medical waste

Other Waste

Miscellaneous bulk waste that does not fit other categories.

  • Broken fixtures
  • Large household waste
  • Mixed disposal items
